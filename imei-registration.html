03 Mercado Libre - IMEI incorporation

Ask for the right detail
at the right time.

Bringing device IMEI collection into the label-generation flow for Global Selling.

Focus
UX writing · Flow design
Validation feedback
Context
Global Selling · Colombia
Customs requirements
My contribution
Copy, collection flow,
validation and alignment
01 / The challenge

Customs information was creating manual work.

Mobile devices required an IMEI as part of the customs process. Collecting it outside the shipping flow could lead to holds and extra manual effort. The experience needed to make the requirement clear and capture a valid 15-digit number as sellers generated labels.

02 / The design response

Build the requirement into the task already underway.

The solution added IMEI collection to label generation, with a clear explanation of why the information was needed, validation in the entry flow, visibility on the printed label and support for bulk printing across multiple shipments.

03 / My contribution

Make compliance understandable and actionable.

  • Wrote concise content explaining the IMEI requirement.
  • Designed the point in the shipping flow where sellers provide the number.
  • Shaped validation and error feedback for the 15-digit entry.
  • Aligned the approach with customs, logistics and engineering partners.
What this work reflects

When a service needs information from someone, the timing and explanation matter as much as the field itself.

04 / The result

Fewer customs delays, faster deliveries.

By adding IMEI capture to label generation, the journey helped reduce customs-related delays and move products toward their destinations sooner.
