01 / The idea

Give repeat travellers a reason to come back.

Holafly Travel Club is a loyalty programme designed to reward customers. Members collect HolaCoins with eSIM purchases and can use them toward future purchases. The experience needed to make the earning and redemption mechanics easy to understand and feel connected to the rest of the product.

02 / My contribution

Make the rewards legible at every step.

  • Designed interfaces for coin balances, earning and membership status.
  • Worked on the flow for earning and redeeming rewards.
  • Developed content and messaging for the programme.
  • Considered responsive layouts across screen sizes.
What this work reflects

A loyalty program only feels valuable when people can understand what they have, how they earned it and what they can do next.
