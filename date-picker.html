02 Holafly · eSIM plan customisation

Let the trip set
the duration.

A more precise way for travellers to choose how long their eSIM plan should last.

Focus
UX research · Product design
UX writing
Problem
Fixed plan durations did not
match every trip
My contribution
Problem framing, picker concept,
copy and PDP updates
01 / The challenge

Travellers had to fit their plans to the product.

Customers could not choose the exact number of days for an eSIM plan. Someone travelling for 13 days had to choose between a 10-day plan that left part of the trip uncovered, or a 15-day plan with two days they would not use.

That mismatch made a simple purchase decision feel like a compromise.

02 / The design response

Make the plan length match the itinerary.

I worked on a date-picker experience that lets travellers select the exact coverage duration they need. The intent was to make the choice feel direct and transparent, while updating the product page content so people could understand the flexibility before they committed.

03 / My contribution

Connect the interaction to clear guidance.

  • Used customer feedback to frame the duration mismatch.
  • Conceptualised the date picker and collaborated on the experience.
  • Wrote supporting copy and tooltips to help explain the choice.
  • Updated product-page messaging to make the flexibility clear.
What this work reflects

Small product choices can shape the whole trip. Giving people the right level of control can make a decision feel much easier.
