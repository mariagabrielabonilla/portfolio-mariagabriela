01 / A different kind of design

Long before product design, I was shaping how people met culture.

As a Publications Editor at Teatro Mayor, I worked across the words and materials that helped audiences, artists and production teams connect with the theatre’s programme. The work ranged from playbills and event publications to educational and digital content.

That experience built a lasting foundation in audience awareness, clear information, editorial craft and close collaboration with graphic designers.

01 / FESTIVAL PUBLICATION

A guide to four days of music

For the V Bogotá International Classical Music Festival, I developed and adapted editorial content for a bilingual brochure covering 31 concerts and more than 40 artists. The work included the festival overview, concert descriptions, schedules and artist biographies.

  • Research & information gathering
  • English–Spanish content
  • Editing & proofing
  • Graphic design collaboration

02 / EDUCATIONAL STORYTELLING

Making the performing arts approachable

For Teatropedia, I researched and wrote scripts for short educational capsules about theatre, dance, music and circus. Where useful, I interviewed subject experts, reviewed drafts with them and supervised video editing so the final story stayed accurate, accessible and engaging.

  • Research & expert interviews
  • Scriptwriting
  • Editorial review
  • Video production collaboration

03 / Beyond the page

A connected editorial system across formats.

The role also covered playbills with scripts and production notes; educational materials for workshops and outreach; newsletters, social posts and website updates; and printed programmes, catalogues and promotional pieces. Each format had a different job, but all needed to make the theatre’s programme easy to discover and understand.

At first glance, this might seem like an unexpected project to include in my portfolio, but I genuinely learned SO much from it that leaving it out didn't feel right. Working under pressure, collaborating with a wide range of stakeholders, and navigating different perspectives gave me invaluable hands-on experience. And, of course, seeing everything come together into a meaningful and satisfying result made the whole experience even more rewarding.

What this work reflects

Editorial design is experience design too: every headline, schedule and explanation can help someone feel welcome in a new world.
